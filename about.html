About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Riverbend Yoga Studio is for people who want the benefits of yoga without the pressure. Our instructors start from where each student is and explain every pose in plain language.

Meet our instructors

Maya Collins, Lead Instructor

Maya leads our Vinyasa Flow and Steady Strength classes and oversees instructor training at Riverbend. She began practising yoga to manage the stress of a demanding office job, and she still remembers how awkward her first class felt. She still teaches with that in mind and never expects a pose to look a certain way.

Teaches: Vinyasa Flow, Steady Strength Vinyasa, private sessions

Daniel Reyes, Gentle and Restorative Yoga

Daniel specialises in slow, supportive classes for students who are new to yoga, recovering from injury, or simply in need of rest. His Restorative Yoga evenings are the quietest hours of the week at the studio. Daniel is also our go-to instructor for students who would like help adapting poses.

Teaches: Gentle Yoga, Restorative Yoga, Beginner Basics

Grace Okafor, Chair Yoga and older adults

Grace has spent many years teaching yoga to older adults and to students with limited mobility. Her Chair Yoga classes focus on balance and moving with confidence in daily life. She often stays after class to answer questions.

Teaches: Chair Yoga, Gentle Yoga, Lunchtime Stretch

Accessibility accommodations

Here is what we have in place so more people can practise comfortably:

  • The studio is on the ground floor, with a step-free entrance and an accessible restroom.
  • Chairs, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets are free to use in every class.
  • Instructors show seated and supported versions of poses, so you can practise without getting down to the floor.
  • Instructors describe each movement out loud, so you can follow along without watching closely.
  • Lighting is soft and music is kept low or turned off.
  • A carer or support person can come to class with you at no extra cost.

If you have a specific need, please let us know when you send a request or speak to your instructor before class. You may also prefer to start with a one-to-one private session.

Studio policies

Arrival and check-in

Please arrive 10 minutes before your first class so we can show you around and help you set up. Doors close 5 minutes after class begins so that the room stays calm for everyone.

Cancellations

Please cancel at least 12 hours before a group class or 24 hours before a private session. Late cancellations and missed classes count as one class from your pass.

Health and safety

Please tell your instructor about any injuries, recent surgery, pregnancy, or health conditions before class. If you are unsure whether yoga is right for you, we recommend speaking with your doctor first.

Studio etiquette

  • Please silence your phone before entering the practice room.
  • Shoes stay in the cubbies by the entrance.
  • Please avoid strong perfumes, as some students are sensitive to scents.
  • Wipe down shared mats and props after use with the sprays provided.
