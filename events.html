Workshops and event requests

Once a month, we host a two-hour workshop that explores one topic in more depth than a regular class allows. Workshops are small and open to all levels unless noted.

Monthly workshop list

All workshops take place on Saturdays from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM and cost $35. Places are limited to 14 students, so we recommend requesting a spot early.

Breath and Calm: An Introduction to Breathing Practices

Date: Saturday, November 21, 2026
Instructor: Daniel Reyes
Level: All levels, beginners welcome

Learn simple breathing techniques you can use at your desk, before sleep, or whenever you feel overwhelmed. Includes a guided seated meditation.

Yoga for Desk Workers: Neck, Shoulders and Back

Date: Saturday, December 12, 2026
Instructor: Grace Okafor
Level: All levels

A practical workshop for busy professionals. You will leave with a short routine to ease tension from long hours of sitting and screen time.

New Year, Gentle Start: Building a Home Practice

Date: Saturday, January 16, 2027
Instructor: Maya Collins
Level: Beginners

Create a simple 15-minute yoga routine you can practise at home, and learn how to stay consistent without pushing too hard.

Balance and Stability for Older Adults

Date: Saturday, February 13, 2027
Instructor: Grace Okafor
Level: All levels, chairs provided

Gentle standing and chair-supported practice for steadier balance and stronger legs.

Arm Balance Foundations: Preparing for Crow Pose

Date: Saturday, March 13, 2027
Instructor: Maya Collins
Level: Some yoga experience recommended

Break down crow pose step by step, with plenty of supported variations using blocks. Lifting off the floor is optional; the preparation work is the real goal.

Deep Rest: Restorative Yoga and Yoga Nidra

Date: Saturday, April 17, 2027
Instructor: Daniel Reyes
Level: All levels

An afternoon of fully supported restorative poses followed by a guided deep-relaxation practice. Bring comfortable layers.
