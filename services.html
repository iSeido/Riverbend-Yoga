Class types

Gentle Yoga

Level: Beginner friendly
Length: 60 minutes

A slow, steady class that focuses on easy stretches, joint-friendly movement, and relaxed breathing. Gentle Yoga is our most popular starting point for first-time students and anyone returning to exercise.

Beginner Basics

Level: New students
Length: 60 minutes

Each Saturday, Beginner Basics breaks down the foundational poses step by step. You will learn how to use props, how to adjust poses safely, and what common yoga terms mean, so you can join any class with confidence.

Vinyasa Flow

Level: All levels, some experience helpful
Length: 60 minutes

A flowing class that links each movement with the breath. Instructors offer simpler and stronger variations throughout, so you can choose how much challenge you want on any given day. Our featured Steady Strength Vinyasa class builds toward one focus pose each week.

Restorative Yoga

Level: All levels
Length: 60 minutes

A deeply relaxing evening class. Poses are fully supported by bolsters, blankets, and blocks and held for several minutes, helping you release tension and prepare for a good night's sleep.

Chair Yoga

Level: All levels, ideal for older adults
Length: 60 minutes

All poses are practised seated or standing with the support of a chair. It helps with balance and posture, and you never have to get down to the floor.

Lunchtime Stretch

Level: All levels
Length: 45 minutes

Designed for busy professionals, this short weekday class targets the neck, shoulders, hips, and lower back. You can attend in comfortable work clothes and still be back at your desk on time.

See when each class runs in our weekly schedule overview.

Private sessions

Private sessions give you one-to-one time with a Riverbend instructor in our studio. They are a good choice if you:

  • Are completely new to yoga and would like a gentle introduction before joining a group class.
  • Are working around an injury, a health condition, or limited mobility.
  • Have a specific goal, such as better posture, improved balance, or a steady home practice.
  • Prefer a schedule that fits around shift work or travel.

Your first private session begins with a short conversation about your goals and any physical considerations, so your instructor can plan a practice that is safe and comfortable for you. Sessions are available for one person or for two people practising together.
