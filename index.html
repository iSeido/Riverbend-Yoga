Our mission

We opened Riverbend for people who like the idea of yoga but feel out of place in crowded, fast-paced classes. Whether you are stepping onto a mat for the first time or coming back after years away, you will find a quiet room and a teacher who starts from where you are today.

Classes are capped at 14 students and move at an easy pace, so the instructor has time to help each person.

New to yoga? You belong here

Many of our students arrive feeling nervous, stiff, or unsure what to expect. That is completely normal, and our instructors are here to help.

  • You do not need any experience. Every class includes simpler options, and Gentle Yoga and Restorative Yoga are planned with beginners in mind.
  • You do not need to be flexible. That comes with practice.
  • We lend mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and chairs in every class, free of charge.
  • You can stop and rest whenever you need to.
  • Your first group class is free, so you can try us before choosing a pass.

If you have an injury, a health condition, or mobility needs, read about our accessibility accommodations or speak with your instructor before class.

Featured class: Steady Strength Vinyasa

This month's featured class is Steady Strength Vinyasa, a 60-minute flowing class that links breath with movement. Each session works up to one focus pose, such as the crow pose in the photo, and every step has an easier version. Some students lift off the floor. Plenty stay with the blocks and the preparation work, and that is just as useful.

Best for students who have taken a few classes and want a gentle challenge. Learn more about all of our class types.
